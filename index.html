Questions parents ask

Is this available now?

Not yet. We're gauging interest before we build the trailer. If enough families ask about dates, we plan to open bookings in early 2027 and will reach out to you first.

What ages is it for?

It's designed for kids 2 to 8. Toddlers get their own Hatchling Hunt nook, and older kids run the diggers, the haul relay and the magnet mine.

Who watches the kids?

Our Crew Chief runs the trailer the whole time. A parent or guardian still needs to stay at the party.

How much space do you need?

Roughly a long driveway or street spot for a truck and trailer, on flat ground. We'll confirm exact sizes before launch.

Will there be sand everywhere?

The sand stays in the pit and trays inside the trailer, and there's a brush-off mat at the door. We do the cleanup.

What about the heat, or rain?

That's the idea: the play room is enclosed and shaded, and we plan to air-condition it. Light rain is fine. If there's lightning or a storm warning, we'll reschedule with you.
